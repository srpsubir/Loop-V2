# Loop GTM Launch Plan

**ICP:** English-speaking expats in EU and SE Asia
**Platform:** Mac (Windows next, Mobile after validation)
**Monetization:** Freemium (limited chapters) + subscription

---

## The Engine

Loop's growth engine is a social loop layered over a habit loop.

**Habit loop (primary):** Expat opens Loop → gets one person + a reason → sends the message → feels good → comes back tomorrow. The daily prompt IS the retention mechanic.

**Social loop (secondary — must be designed in):** Expat uses Loop → mentions it to another expat from a shared chapter → they install it. Currently accidental. Needs to be made structural.

**The big unlock — a "Wrapped" moment:** A beautiful visual summary of your chapters, how many people you've stayed close to, which chapters of your life they represent. Shareable on Instagram/LinkedIn. For expats who are identity-driven about their journey, this spreads on its own. Should be on the roadmap before public launch.

---

## Stage 1: Alpha (Friends & Family)

**Goal: Learning, not growing.**

### What you're trying to answer
- When does the aha moment actually hit? (First chapter mapped? First brief opened? First message sent?)
- How many steps between install and aha moment? Every extra step kills conversion.
- Do people actually send the WhatsApp message after seeing the brief? This is your core value confirmation.
- What's D7 retention? If below 30%, the habit loop isn't working.

### Exit criteria (don't move to beta until you hit these)
- 15-20 active users with minimum 3 weeks of data
- Clear aha moment identified and validated qualitatively
- At least one user says something equivalent to "I would be genuinely disappointed if this disappeared"
- Single biggest onboarding drop-off point identified

### Alpha design
- Personal onboarding for every user — sit with them (video call or in-person) for the first session. Watch where they get confused.
- Collect feedback via voice notes or WhatsApp, not forms. Friends respond to friends.
- Give each alpha user 3 invite codes to share with people from their own chapters. Tests the social mechanic before you formalize it AND gets you users with overlapping networks.

### Fix needed before beta
The "no email, we'll find you" waitlist is charming copy, but you're burning future marketing leverage. You need email capture for beta onwards. Even first name + email is enough. Without it, you have no re-engagement path if someone drops off.

## Stage 2: Beta (Controlled Expansion)

**Goal: Hit the Sean Ellis signal with a specific expat cohort.**

### Gate from Alpha
- Clear aha moment identified
- D7 retention above 30%
- At least 5 users have described the product with genuine emotional language (not "it's useful" — "I messaged someone I hadn't spoken to in 2 years")

### ICP focus for Beta
Don't go broad across all English-speaking expats. Go deep on one cluster first. Recommended: **Indian expats in Singapore or UK.** Reasons: massive expat community, WhatsApp-native, Mac ownership is high among this professional cohort, English-speaking, emotionally connected to the "chapters of life" framing.

**Beta size:** 100-300 users. Managed, not viral. Invite-only still. Enough for the Sean Ellis survey (~50 responses minimum) without losing the ability to talk to users.

### Beta distribution tactics
- Direct outreach to expat community admins (WhatsApp groups, Facebook groups) — offer early access as a community, not individuals
- Reddit: r/expats, r/Singapore, r/UKExpats, r/BritishExpats — genuine participation, not promotion. Post about the problem, not the product.
- Internations — find local chapter admins and offer them early access
- LinkedIn — search "expat" + city combinations; reach out personally

### Freemium gate timing (critical)
Don't gate before the aha moment. Free tier = **2 full chapters** + preview of additional chapters (blurred, with a CTA). The preview of other chapters is essential — it shows the value before asking for money. Gating at 1 chapter means many expats won't see enough value; the magic is in seeing all chapters mapped together.

### Beta exit criteria
- 40%+ Sean Ellis score
- Understanding of which user segment has the highest retention (this tells you who to prioritize at launch)

## Stage 3: Launch

### Turbo boosts (one-offs, not the engine)
- **Product Hunt** — expat audience + privacy-first angle is a strong combo there
- **Expat press** — The Local, Expat Focus, Internations blog — earned media at zero cost
- **LinkedIn launch post** from your personal brand (you have an audience; use it)
- **One well-chosen creator partnership** — an expat YouTuber or Substack writer with 10-50k audience outperforms a larger general creator

### Organic engine
By launch, the "Wrapped" moment should be live. This is the highest-value organic distribution mechanic.

---

## Risks (Ranked by Severity)

### 1. Activation friction — biggest conversion killer
"Connect WhatsApp once" requires users to scan a QR code and trust a local app reading their messages. Many will bail here. The privacy copy needs to appear *inside the onboarding flow* right before the connection step, not just on the landing page.

### 2. Mac-only TAM constraint
Fine for alpha and beta. But English-speaking expats in SE Asia skew Android more than the EU cohort. Windows validation needs to happen before scaling in that geography.

### 3. Freemium gate before aha moment
If someone only has 1 chapter mapped and hits the paywall, they leave. Free tier must be generous enough that the aha moment fires before asking for money.

### 4. Cold start data quality
Chapter mapping quality depends on WhatsApp history depth. New phone, recently cleared chats, or low-messaging users will get thin mapping. Need a fallback: let users manually add people and assign them to chapters. Don't rely entirely on inferred data.

### 5. No re-engagement path currently
No email = if a user goes quiet, no way to bring them back. Fix this for beta.

## The One Thing to Build Before Beta

A referral mechanic — not a growth hack, a structural one. Every user gets 3 invites to give to people from their own chapters. This:
- Tests the social loop
- Seeds the network with overlapping contacts (better chapter mapping data)
- Makes the "chapters" framing emotionally resonant before launch

The copy writes itself: *"Invite the people from your chapters to close the loop."*
