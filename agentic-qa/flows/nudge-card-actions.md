# Flow: Nudge card actions

## Objective
Interact with a NudgeCard on the main feed and confirm both primary actions work and read correctly.

## Steps
1. From "Your Loop", locate a NudgeCard.
2. Confirm card surface is the dark card color (#2C1C14) and body text reads at ~70% Ground opacity, legible.
3. Tap "Remind me later" — confirm it dismisses/reschedules and the ~55% Ground opacity label is still readable.
4. Trigger the card's primary action (reach out) — confirm it leads to the correct WhatsApp/Story path.
5. Confirm no more than 2 Terra color uses appear on this screen.

## What to flag
- Low-contrast or illegible text.
- "Remind me later" not actually rescheduling.
- Primary CTA leading to the wrong destination or a dead end.
- Any layout regressions from the spring-transition/skeleton work.
