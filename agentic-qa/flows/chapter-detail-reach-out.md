# Flow: Chapter detail — reach out to crew

## Objective
Open a chapter, confirm all crew members are reachable (not just fading members), and complete a reach-out to WhatsApp.

## Steps
1. From "Your Loop", open any chapter's detail screen ("Their world" for a person, or the chapter view for a group).
2. Confirm fading members are visually distinct (per locked design — fading members get visual prominence).
3. Tap a non-fading crew member. Confirm it still leads to a Story → WhatsApp reach-out path (this must work for ALL crew, not just fading ones).
4. Tap a fading crew member. Confirm the same reach-out path works.
5. Confirm the "On your mind" section (MAV-201) never renders the word "Brief" anywhere — CTAs should read "Their world" / "from their world".

## What to flag
- Any crew member who is NOT tappable/reachable.
- Missing or incorrect visual distinction for fading members.
- Contrast issues (avatar rings should be solid Terra Person #D4804A, max 2 Terra uses per screen).
- Any "Brief" copy remnants.
