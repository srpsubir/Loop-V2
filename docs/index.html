How it works
1

Connect WhatsApp once

Loop connects to WhatsApp the same way WhatsApp Web does. Locally, on your Mac. It maps your closest relationships without uploading anything, anywhere.

2

Loop finds your people

Groups your contacts by the chapter of life you shared: the university years, the old city, the first job. Knows who you haven't spoken to in a while, and why it might matter.

3

You know who to reach out to

One person a day. A reason. A brief with the last thing you talked about. One tap to open WhatsApp knowing exactly what to say.

The brief
Not a reminder.
A memory.

When it's time to reach out, Loop shows you the last thing you talked about, the birthday coming up, the shared chapter. You open WhatsApp knowing what to say.

Your conversations stay on your Mac.

  • No cloud. Your WhatsApp history never leaves your laptop.
  • No relay server. Loop talks to WhatsApp Web directly, locally.
  • No Meta dependency. It reads data you already have.
